Enemies
O  Bouncer: bounces around the map
%  Ghost: follows through walls
&  Shooter: fires projectile volleys
#  Turret: shoots in all directions
^  Leaper: jumps toward you
!  Mushroom: hurts nearby tiles
$  Grappler: pulls you in lines
+  Plus turret: moves and shoots straight
x  X turret: moves and shoots diagonally

Other entities
@  Player: you
W  Wall: blocks movement
G  Gate: open 3 gates to clear a level
c  Coin: gives more upgrade choices
*  Projectile: hurts the player
X  Spawner: turns into an enemy after a timer

Pickups
T  Time stop pickup: collect 2 to stop enemies
K  Kill pickup: removes one enemy
S  Swap pickup: teleports to the other swap pickup

Upgrades
1  Increase max health and heal to full
2  Kill the enemy that damages you
3  Heal 1 HP after every stage
4  Heal back to half HP after every stage
5  Spawn 2 time stop pickups
6  Spawn 2 kill enemy pickups
7  Spawn 2 swap pickups
8  Kill an enemy every 2 pickups
9  Gain 1 HP every 2 pickups
10 Loop around the map once
11 Immune every fifth turn
12 Block one projectile per floor
13 Limit projectiles to 12
14 Slow projectiles

Hints
Projectiles
-Projectiles always move in deterministic patterns, and always damage the tile they are going into, not the tile they were previously in. If a projectile is moving toward you and about to hit you, you can dodge the projectile by moving counter to its current trajectory with good timing.

Time
-If you stay in a stage too long, enemies will start to spawn, significantly making the level much harder.

Coins
-If you're having trouble finding good upgrades, try collecting the 'c' coins scattered around the map. More coins collected at the end of each level means more options presented in the upgrades screen.
