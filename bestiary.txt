== Enemies ==
O  Bouncer      Bounces diagonally around the map.
%  Ghost        Follows the player and ignores walls.
&  Shooter      Keeps distance and fires projectile volleys.
#  Turret       Stays still and shoots in all directions.
^  Leaper       Jumps two tiles toward the player, leaving behind a damaging area in its wake.
!  Mushroom     Creates a red danger area nearby.
$  Grappler     Pulls the player in rows or columns.
+  Plus turret  Moves and shoots straight projectiles.
x  X turret     Moves and shoots diagonal projectiles.

== Other Entities ==
@  Player       You.
W  Wall         Blocks player, enemies, and projectiles.
G  Gate         Open 3 gates to clear a level.
c  Coin         Adds upgrade choices after the level.
*  Projectile   Damages the player on contact.
X  Spawner      Turns into an enemy after its timer.

== Pickups ==
T  Time stop    Collect 2 to stop enemies for 3 turns.
K  Kill pickup  Removes one active enemy.
S  Swap pickup  Teleports to the other swap pickup.

== Upgrades ==
1  Increase max health and heal to full.
2  Kill the enemy that damages you.
3  Heal 1 HP after every stage.
4  Start each stage at least half healed.
5  Spawn 2 time stop pickups each stage.
6  Spawn 2 kill enemy pickups each stage.
7  Spawn 2 swap pickups each stage.
8  Kill an enemy every 2 pickups.
9  Gain 1 HP every 2 pickups.
10 Loop around the map once.
11 Become immune every fifth turn.
12 Block one projectile per floor.
13 Limit active projectiles to 12.
14 Slow projectile movement.

== Hints ==
Projectiles:
Projectiles damage the tile they move into.
Move out of their path before the next enemy update.

Time:
If you stay too long, spawners can appear.
Spawners make the level harder by adding enemies.

Coins:
You normally see 2 upgrade choices.
Each coin collected adds 1 more choice that level.
